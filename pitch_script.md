# KrishiMitra — Hackathon Pitch Script & Slide Deck

---

## 🎤 SLIDE 1 — HOOK (30 sec)

> "India feeds 1.4 billion people. But the 600 million farmers who grow that food have no single digital tool to manage their entire farming life. They use 5 different apps, can't read the UI, and can't afford to lose a single day's crop to a disease they didn't know about.

> Today, we fix that."

**Slide visuals:** Full-screen image of a farmer with a phone. Stat: "600M+ rural smartphone users. 0 truly unified agri-apps."

---

## 🎤 SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM (45 sec)

> "Farmers today face three critical gaps:
> 1. **Information** — No real-time crop prices, weather, or disease alerts
> 2. **Access** — Government schemes they're entitled to but never claimed
> 3. **Labor** — Finding workers for harvest season is still done through middlemen

> And every single solution today is fragmented — one app for prices, another for weather, another for selling. That means farmers fall through the cracks."

**Slide visuals:** 3 pain points as icons. Real farmer photos.

---

## 🎤 SLIDE 3 — THE SOLUTION (1 min)

> "KrishiMitra — one app, one AI, everything a farmer needs.

> **[LIVE DEMO — open the app]**

> Watch this: I'm a farmer in Hapur. I open the app, tap the mic, and say: *'Mazdoor chahiye kal ke liye'* — the AI finds available laborers near me, shows me rates, and books one — all in 20 seconds.

> I say *'Gehu ka bhav batao'* — live mandi prices appear.

> I say *'Meri fasal mein kuch dikkat hai'* — I upload a photo, AI diagnoses the disease and gives me a treatment plan."

**Slide visuals:** App screenshots / screen recording of demo.

## 🎤 SLIDE 5 — TECH STACK (30 sec)

> "Built on a modern, production-grade stack:"

- **Frontend:** Flutter (iOS + Android, one codebase)
- **Backend:** Node.js + Express + Prisma ORM
- **Database:** MongoDB Atlas
- **AI:** NVIDIA-hosted GPT with OpenAI function-calling API
- **Voice:** Flutter TTS + Speech-to-Text
- **Maps:** OpenStreetMap (flutter_map)

> "Everything is deployed, everything is real — no mock data."

---

## 🎤 SLIDE 6 — USP & DIFFERENTIATORS (30 sec)

> "What makes us different from every agri-app out there?

> 1. **Agentic AI** — The bot doesn't just answer, it *acts*. It navigates, books, lists, and transacts on your behalf
> 2. **Voice-first, Hindi-first** — Designed for users who can't type
> 3. **All-in-one** — 10 features, one login, one experience
> 4. **Real APIs** — Live market data, real weather, real AI diagnosis"

---

## 🎤 SLIDE 7 — BUSINESS MODEL (30 sec)

> "Three clear revenue streams once we scale:"

1. **Transaction commission (2–5%)** on marketplace and labor bookings
2. **Government/NGO contracts** — NABARD, PM-KISAN digital infra programs pay for agri platforms
3. **Kisan Pro subscription** — Advanced AI advisory, priority listings, yield analytics

> "Base app is free — we monetize on volume, not on farmers."
